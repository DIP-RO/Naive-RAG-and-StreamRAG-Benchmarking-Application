Climate Change and Agriculture

Climate change significantly impacts global agriculture through changing weather patterns, increased frequency of extreme events, and shifting growing seasons. Key effects include:

Temperature Rise: Average global temperatures have increased by 1.1°C since pre-industrial times. This affects crop yields, with wheat yields declining by 6% per degree Celsius of warming.

Water Scarcity: Changing precipitation patterns lead to droughts in some regions and floods in others. Agriculture accounts for 70% of global freshwater use.

Adaptation Strategies:
- Drought-resistant crop varieties developed through genetic modification
- Precision agriculture using IoT sensors and AI
- Improved irrigation systems and water management
- Crop diversification and rotation
- Soil conservation techniques like no-till farming

Mitigation approaches include carbon sequestration through regenerative agriculture, reducing fertilizer use, and adopting renewable energy on farms.
