Weather Patterns and Forecasting

Modern weather forecasting uses a combination of satellite data, ground stations, and computer models to predict atmospheric conditions. The World Meteorological Organization coordinates global data sharing.

Key Concepts:
- Temperature is measured in Celsius or Fahrenheit
- Wind speed is measured in km/h or knots
- Precipitation includes rain, snow, sleet, and hail
- Atmospheric pressure is measured in millibars (mb) or hectopascals (hPa)

Weather APIs like Open-Meteo provide free access to forecast data. They use numerical weather prediction models that solve mathematical equations describing atmospheric behavior.

Climate is different from weather: climate describes long-term patterns (30+ years), while weather describes short-term conditions (hours to days).
